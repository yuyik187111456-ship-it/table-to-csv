Privacy Policy

Table to CSV & Excel does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or any data from the pages you visit.

All table extraction and file generation happens locally in your browser. No data leaves your device, and no analytics or tracking is used.

Permissions are used only to function:

  • Downloads —save the generated CSV file
